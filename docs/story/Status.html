Flora of Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound

To date, 1193 vascular plant species have been recorded in the Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound Biosphere Region, drawing together 66611 records from historical collections, iNaturalist observations and other sources. Each species falls into one of three groups: confirmed species, reported historically and recorded again in recent searches; historical species, reported in the past but not yet found again; and new species, first reported in the region in recent years.
