Community contributions to our knowledge of the regional plant diversity

Through the combined efforts of 3464 individuals, the Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound naturalist community has now documented a total of 1179 vascular plant species. Since 2000, 83 percent of the historically reported plant diversity has been confirmed and 486 new species reports added to the list.

Community members have now documented 84 percent of the total plant diversity known to the region on iNaturalist.

Contribute to this growing record by submitting your observations to the Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound Biosphere Region iNaturalist project.
