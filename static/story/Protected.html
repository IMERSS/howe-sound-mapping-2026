Vascular plant diversity within Howe Sound’s protected areas

Map 4. Protected Areas

Based on our current knowledge, 72 percent of the native plant diversity known to Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound has been reported within protected areas. While this preliminary evaluation is limited due to biases in sampling effort and other deficiencies in the data, it represents a starting point for a more thorough assessment of progress being made toward goals of conserving our regional habitat diversity.
